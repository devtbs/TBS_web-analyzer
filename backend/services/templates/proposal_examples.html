05 · What good enough SEO misses

The trap most agencies still sell in 2026

Modern SEO extends the same logic to AI surfaces - “good enough” stops working once AI becomes the first stop.

Old SEO (the trap)

  • Rank on Google for “wellness resort Switzerland”
  • Drive traffic to the booking page
  • Convert on-site
  • Done
This is what most agencies still sell in 2026.

What actually wins the booking

  • Be cited when AI recommends Swiss resorts
  • Be named in comparison answers
  • Have structured facts AI can lift into responses
  • Be trusted across Reddit, Quora, forums AI reads
  • Appear in future training data with favorable context
Same fundamentals, broader definition of “ranking.”
Modern SEO isn't about keywords. It's about being cited, trusted, and chosen by the systems travelers now ask first.

08 · Old SEO vs Modern SEO

Same craft. Different surface.

The technical foundations don't change. What changes is the distribution: AI agents now read your website the same way Google did ten years ago.

5 - 10 years ago

  • Stuff the keyword “wellness resort Switzerland” 50 times
  • Make the homepage look attractive
  • Optimize for Google visibility only
  • Buy backlinks from any directory that would publish them
  • Measure: ranking position

Now

  • Write valuable articles: “Why a Swiss Alps wellness retreat is worth the journey”, “What does a 5-star spa program actually include?”, “Best time of year for a lake-view wellness stay”
  • Create content that answers questions at every stage of the journey
  • Optimize so Google + ChatGPT + Gemini + Perplexity all understand your expertise and recognize your brand
  • Earn citations from authoritative travel, wellness, Swiss hospitality publications
  • Measure: AI mentions, citations, direct bookings
Same craft. Different surface. Crawlability, structured data, content depth, authority signals - none of this changes. What changes is the distribution: AI agents now read your website the same way Google did ten years ago.

✅ What's working
  • Real organic traffic - 7.9K visits per month
  • Real keyword footprint - 5.5K ranking terms
  • Authority signals - 573 referring domains
  • Strong brand among returning Swiss guests
  • 21 Aug update: AIO traffic 3×'d to ~60/mo - channel exists, not yet commercial
❌ What's missing in AI
  • Brand score across 5 LLMs: 5 / 100
  • Visible in only 2.4% of prompts
  • Of 26 prompts assessed: 25 returned 0% visibility. Only one branded prompt (queries mentioning Panorama by name) yielded recognition, at 20% visibility. The 25 unbranded prompts - the kind real travelers use - return zero citations.
  • Category queries (Swiss resorts, Zurich wellness): zero mentions
  • Not in the top 20 Swiss luxury hotels cited by AI
So the issue is not effort. The site has authority, content, and rankings. The issue is that AI indexes favor HTML pages over PDFs - several core pages (spa menu, rooms overview, culinary brochure) currently exist only as PDFs, which means AI can't read them. Converting those PDFs to indexable HTML pages is one of the highest-leverage quick wins.
